Meet Vasudha — Founder & Fashion Designer, Designer Vastra

After graduating with honors in Fashion & Apparel Design from the National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT), I noticed a disheartening gap: ready-to-wear labels forced women into rigid, unyielding sizes, while luxury couture studios charged exorbitant markups with zero personal connection to the actual maker.

I founded this independent atelier to return to the intimacy of vintage Indian tailoring. When you commission a garment with me, you speak directly to the designer who cuts your pattern. No floor salespeople, no opaque factories. From sourcing pure Banarasi katan from master weavers to mapping neckline depths that flatter your collarbones, every creation is treated as a bespoke heirloom.
