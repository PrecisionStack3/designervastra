Questions & Concerns

Frequently Asked Questions

Send us a quick photo of your fabric on WhatsApp. Vasudha will review necklines, trims, and can-can requirements with you within 30 minutes.

Can I provide my own fabric or do you source it?

expand_more

You are completely welcome to provide your own fabric! Whether it is a pure Kanjeevaram wedding saree, ancestral Banarasi silk, or unstitched fabric bought from another designer, we treat it with supreme care. If you prefer us to source, we have direct weaver tie-ups in Banaras, Kanchipuram, and Chanderi to procure authentic pure silks at wholesale prices.

What if I am outside your city or living abroad (USA, UK, UAE)?

expand_more

Nearly 40% of our bridal clientele reside in the US, UK, Canada, and the Middle East! We coordinate via scheduled WhatsApp video consultations where Designer Aaranya guides you step-by-step with a measuring tape. Once stitched, we ship through DHL Express / FedEx with door-to-door insurance and tracked delivery in 4-6 business days.

How do I take measurements if we can't meet in person?

expand_more

You have two foolproof options: (1) Courier your absolute best-fitting blouse or kurti to our atelier. We will replicate its exact fit lines and safely send it back with your finished outfit. (2) Book a complimentary 20-minute video measurement session where we guide a friend or family member to measure your 18 key anatomical points using simple household measuring tape.

Do you do emergency 48-to-72 hour stitching for unexpected wedding invites?

expand_more

Yes. We reserve two master tailor machines specifically for urgent festive emergencies. Express turnaround (48 to 72 hours) carries a flat ₹800 priority surcharge. Please connect directly via our designer WhatsApp line before couriering to confirm immediate slot availability.

What happens if the outfit doesn't fit me right?

expand_more

Under our 100% Aaranya Fit Guarantee, you have 7 calendar days after delivery to notify us of any tightness, loose darts, or neckline adjustments. We arrange free return pickup, do the precision alteration at zero extra charge, and dispatch it back to you within 48 hours.
